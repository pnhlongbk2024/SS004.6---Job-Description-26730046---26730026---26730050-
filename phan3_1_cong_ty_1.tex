\subsection{Công ty 1: FPT Software}

\textbf{Job Title:} AI Engineer (LLM / Generative AI) \\
\textbf{Link tuyển dụng:} \url{https://career.fpt-software.com/jobs/ai-engineer}

\subsubsection*{Job Description (Nguyên gốc tiếng Anh)}

\textbf{About FPT Software:} \\
\indent FPT Software, a subsidiary of FPT Group, is a leading global IT service provider headquartered in Vietnam. With 33,000+ employees in 88 offices across 30 countries, we serve 1,100+ clients, including 96 Fortune 500 companies.

\paragraph{Responsibilities (Trách nhiệm công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Research, design, and implement artificial intelligence solutions leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI technologies.
    \item Design and build Prompt Engineering pipelines and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures to integrate domain-specific knowledge into LLM systems.
    \item Evaluate, test, and improve AI model performance, ensuring accuracy, reliability, and production readiness.
    \item Integrate AI capabilities and services (such as Azure AI, OpenAI APIs) into enterprise applications and existing business workflows.
    \item Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Software Engineers, Solution Architects, Data Analysts) to convert business requirements into technical AI solutions.
    \item Document technical designs, workflows, and AI solution architectures clearly.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Requirements (Yêu cầu công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Information Technology, or related fields.
    \item At least 2 years of experience in AI/ML engineering or related roles.
    \item Experience working with Large Language Models (LLMs), prompt engineering, and RAG pipelines, especially on Azure AI services.
    \item Proficiency in Python programming and key libraries/frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, OpenAI API, PyTorch, or TensorFlow).
    \item Experience deploying AI solutions in cloud environments (Azure preferred, AWS/GCP is a plus).
    \item Strong problem-solving skills, analytical mindset, and ability to work effectively in dynamic, global project environments.
    \item Good written and verbal English communication skills.
\end{itemize}
