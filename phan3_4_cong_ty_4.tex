\subsection{Công ty 4: VNG Corporation}

\textbf{Job Title:} Lead AI Engineer \\
\textbf{Link tuyển dụng:} \url{https://www.itjobs.com.vn/vi/job/82402/lead-ai-engineer}

\subsubsection*{Job Description (Nguyên gốc tiếng Anh)}

\textbf{About VNG Corporation:} \\
\indent VNG Corporation is a pioneer of Vietnam's internet industry, founded in 2004, with a mission to use technology to improve the lives of Vietnamese people through products spanning social platforms, fintech (ZaloPay), cloud (VNG Cloud/GreenNode), and gaming.

\paragraph{Responsibilities (Trách nhiệm công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Provide technical guidance and strategic vision as Team Leader for the LLM Project team.
    \item Support the professional growth and development of team members.
    \item Ensure timely project completion while meeting quality benchmarks.
    \item Monitor industry innovations in AI, NLP, and LLM applications.
    \item Collaborate across departments to integrate AI solutions into the overall system architecture.
    \item Manage project timelines and resources.
    \item Foster a team culture that emphasizes innovation and continuous learning.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Requirements (Yêu cầu công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
    \item At least 3 years of experience in project management, particularly in software development.
    \item Hands-on background with Large Language Models (LLM) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
    \item Experience with chatbot or conversational AI development.
    \item Strong communication and leadership abilities; team management and motivation capacity.
    \item Adaptability in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
    \item English proficiency at an intermediate level (speaking, reading, writing).
\end{itemize}
