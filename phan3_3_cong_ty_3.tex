\subsection{Công ty 3: VinAI Research}

\noindent\textbf{Job Title:} AI Engineer (Applied AI Engineer) \\
\textbf{Link tuyển dụng:} \url{https://www.vinai.io/recruitment-posts/ai-engineer/}

\subsubsection*{Job Description (Nguyên gốc tiếng Anh)}

\textbf{About VinAI:} \\
\indent VinAI is a fast-growing AI research and technology company (member of Vingroup) based in Hanoi, Vietnam, working on cutting-edge Computer Vision, Large Language Model, and robotics research and translating it into real-world products.

\paragraph{Responsibilities (Trách nhiệm công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Collaborate with computer vision researchers on cutting-edge algorithms for tasks including activity recognition, image classification, object detection/tracking, semantic segmentation, and scene understanding.
    \item Translate research ideas into production-ready computer vision and machine learning solutions that address real-world problems.
    \item Develop prototypes and products based on the above research.
    \item Execute data collection, annotation management, and experimentation; support scientific research publication efforts.
    \item Conduct independent research and implement contemporary machine learning methodologies.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Requirements (Yêu cầu công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Robotics, or related disciplines.
    \item Proficiency with deep learning frameworks and architectures (CNN, Transformer models).
    \item Computer vision expertise: 2D/3D object detection, object tracking, classification, image segmentation.
    \item Strong programming skills in C/C++, Python, and CUDA; experience with edge device deployment is preferred.
    \item At least 2-3 years of experience in Machine Learning and Computer Vision; Kaggle competition credentials are a plus.
    \item Knowledge of software engineering best practices.
\end{itemize}
