\subsection{Công ty 7: Amazon Web Services (AWS)}

\textbf{Job Title:} Delivery Consultant - AI/ML, Data \& Machine Learning (DML) \\
\textbf{Link tuyển dụng:} \url{https://www.amazon.jobs/en/jobs/10394713/machine-learning-engineer-data-machine-learning-dml}

\subsubsection*{Job Description (Nguyên gốc tiếng Anh)}

\textbf{About the Role:} \\
\indent AWS Professional Services is seeking a Machine Learning Engineer to work directly with customers to design, evangelize, implement, and scale AI/ML solutions, emphasizing applying generative AI to real-world business challenges while guiding customers through their AI transformation journey.

\paragraph{Responsibilities (Trách nhiệm công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Design and deploy scalable AI/ML solutions on AWS, including model selection and fine-tuning.
    \item Develop machine learning and generative AI applications that address real business problems.
    \item Identify high-value use cases and propose implementation strategies together with customer stakeholders.
    \item Provide technical guidance on responsible, cost-efficient AI/ML application.
    \item Serve as a trusted advisor on emerging AI technologies and best practices.
    \item Mentor team members and create reusable AI/ML artifacts.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Requirements (Yêu cầu công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Bachelor's degree in a STEM field.
    \item Professional software engineering experience with proficiency across the full development lifecycle.
    \item 3+ years of experience in machine learning, statistical modeling, or data analysis.
    \item Preferred: Master's degree in a STEM field; multi-team, cross-disciplinary project experience; GenAI model performance benchmarking experience; proficiency in Python, SQL/NoSQL, and API development; Large Language Models and prompt engineering expertise.
\end{itemize}
