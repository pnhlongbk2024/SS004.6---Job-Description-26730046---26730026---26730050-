\subsection{Công ty 7: Ngân hàng TMCP Kỹ Thương Việt Nam (Techcombank)}

\noindent\textbf{Job Title:} Senior / Expert AI Engineer (IT Division / Techcom Life) \\
\textbf{Link tuyển dụng:} \url{https://www.topcv.vn/brand/techcombank/tuyen-dung/senior-expert-ai-engineer-j2142217.html}

\subsubsection*{Job Description (Nguyên gốc tiếng Anh)}

\textbf{About the Role (Mô tả chung):} \\
\indent The Expert AI Engineer plays a pivotal role in leading the development of cutting-edge AI applications, specializing in the creation of RAG agentic systems and the fine-tuning of large-scale LLMs and VLMs. This role drives the strategic direction of AI initiatives and mentors teams to develop advanced AI capabilities that solve complex financial/banking problems.

\paragraph{Responsibilities (Trách nhiệm công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Lead the development team to project delivery by providing technical guidance throughout the software development lifecycle.
    \item Define AI strategies and work closely with business stakeholders to ensure AI solutions align with organizational goals.
    \item Design and optimize AI models (LLMs, VLMs, RAG systems) for scalability, performance, and efficiency.
    \item Provide technical leadership and mentorship to junior and senior AI engineers, fostering a culture of innovation.
    \item Conduct deep research and development to explore new AI technologies and methodologies.
    \item Oversee the integration of AI solutions with existing business systems and data infrastructure, ensuring smooth deployment and operation.
    \item Define AI vision and roadmap for the organization in collaboration with leadership.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Requirements (Yêu cầu công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Bachelor's/Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, or related technical fields.
    \item Minimum 3--5 years of working experience in software development, AI/ML, or Data Engineering.
    \item Proficiency in Python, Java, or Scala with solid experience in building data pipelines (SQL, Spark, Airflow).
    \item Strong hands-on experience with AI/ML \& LLM frameworks (OpenAI, Hugging Face, LangChain, PyTorch, TensorFlow).
    \item Experience building RAG agentic systems, AI Agents, or automated workflows.
    \item Hands-on experience with Cloud services (AWS: EC2, S3, Lambda, Glue, EKS, etc.) and system architecture (microservices, event-driven architecture, Kafka, Docker, Kubernetes).
    \item Strong problem-solving, system design, and English communication skills.
\end{itemize}
