\subsection{Công ty 5: Grab}

\textbf{Job Title:} Senior Machine Learning Engineer (GrabMart) \\
\textbf{Link tuyển dụng:} \url{https://www.grab.careers/en/jobs/744000119563066/senior-machine-learning-engineer/}

\subsubsection*{Job Description (Nguyên gốc tiếng Anh)}

\textbf{About Grab:} \\
\indent Grab is Southeast Asia's leading superapp, offering ride-hailing, food and grocery delivery, and financial services, leveraging technology and AI to economically empower the region.

\paragraph{Responsibilities (Trách nhiệm công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Analyze large-scale transactional, behavioral, and geospatial datasets to understand shopping patterns.
    \item Build AI-driven products such as conversational shopping assistants, predictive models for order cancellations, and hyper-local demand forecasting.
    \item Implement optimization algorithms that balance demand and supply in real time.
    \item Develop production-grade models with A/B testing and continuous monitoring.
    \item Stay current with AI/ML advancements and contribute to internal intellectual property.
    \item Partner with Engineering, Product, and Analytics teams to scale data-driven solutions regionally.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Requirements (Yêu cầu công việc):}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Master's degree in Machine Learning, Statistics, Computer Science, Economics, or a related quantitative field.
    \item 5+ years of experience as a Data Scientist/Machine Learning Engineer in e-commerce, quick-commerce, or marketplace domains.
    \item Deep Machine Learning/Deep Learning knowledge with proficiency in TensorFlow or PyTorch.
    \item Strong Python or Scala programming expertise.
    \item Experience with MLOps and CI/CD pipelines.
    \item Preferred: experience with recommender systems or supply-chain optimization, big data tools (Spark, Flink, Kafka), and LLM fine-tuning/agentic development (LangChain, AutoGPT, CrewAI).
\end{itemize}
